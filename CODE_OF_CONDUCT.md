# Code of Conduct

We want a constructive, professional community for maintainers, contributors,
and users. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical
feedback, and protect others' privacy and security.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing
private information, or pressuring anyone to disclose credentials or security
details publicly.

For a conduct concern, contact the project maintainers through the private
security-reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled
as privately as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions,
or restrict participation to protect the community.
